Proof-of-Claim - return and refund flow prototype

When a shopper reports a damaged or wrong item, a claim risk score decides how much proof the flow asks for. Most honest shoppers get an instant refund; only riskier claims get live in-app capture, doorstep inspection or review. Concept prototype for a personal product case study, not affiliated with any company.
