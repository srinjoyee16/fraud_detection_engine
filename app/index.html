Proof-of-Claim: working prototype

File a "damaged / wrong item" claim. The app scores the claim with a model running in your browser, picks how much proof it needs, captures that proof (live camera for risky claims, with a random prompt), fingerprints the photo, checks it against earlier claims for near-duplicates, and produces a claim record. Everything stays on this device. The model is trained on synthetic data, so it is for demonstration only.
